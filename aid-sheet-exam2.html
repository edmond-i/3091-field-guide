Bond type & strength

% ionic = 100·[1 − exp(−Δχ²/4)]   μ = δ·r

Stronger bond: smaller atoms, higher bond order (C≡C > C=C > C–C; HF > HCl > HBr > HI)
Ionic solid: brittle (shift → like charges repel), conducts only molten/dissolved. Covalent network: hard, high mp. Molecular: low mp (IMF).

Lewis recipe

① valence e⁻ (+1 per − charge) ② centre = least EN, H never ③ singles, octets outside, leftovers on centre ④ FC = V − L − B/2 ⑤ expanded octet only period ≥ 3 (C,N,O,F never); BF₃, AlH₃, BeF₂ electron-poor ⑥ resonance = average

Hybridization & σ/π

groups on centre: 2 sp · 3 sp² · 4 sp³ · 5 sp³d · 6 sp³d² (lone pairs count; double/triple = 1 group)
σ: 1 per bond (head-on); π: bond order − 1 (side-on p). Triple = 1σ + 2π. #hybrids = #AOs mixed. Label gives parent shape, not exact angle (NH₃ 107°, H₂O 104.5°).
C₂H₄: C sp², HOMO = π, LUMO = π*. C₂H₂: C sp.

Bands & conductivity

n AOs → n levels → band. Metal: partly filled or overlapping (Na ½ full; Mg, Be: s–p overlap). Semiconductor: full VB, small gap ~1 eV. Insulator: gap > ~3 eV.
Eg(eV): C 5.4 · Si 1.1 · Ge 0.67 · Sn 0.08 · Pb 0 (decreases down a group). Longer/weaker bonds → smaller gap (tensile strain).
σ = 1/ρ (S/m; Ω·m). Cu, Ag, Au (s¹d¹⁰) best; alloys worse. Metals: σ ↓ with T. Semiconductors: σ ↑ with T (e⁻ and holes carry).
Photon crosses gap if hν ≥ Eg; LED λ = 1240/Eg (Si 1127 nm IR; GaN 365 nm UV). Work function KE = hν − φ.

Doping

n-type: dopant has more valence e⁻ than the atom it replaces (group 15 in Si; Te) → donor level just below CB. p-type: fewer (group 13: B Al Ga; Zn; Ca on Ga) → acceptor level just above VB (holes).
Pure Si ≈ 1 carrier/10¹² atoms; doped 1 per 10²–10⁷. Dopant fixed, carrier mobile. Too much dopant: saturation + scattering.
Dopant mass: atoms = (ρ/M)·NA·V → ÷ (atoms per carrier, e.g. 10⁵) → ÷NA × Mdopant.
Compound: III–V, II–VI average 4 e⁻. Eg: GaN 3.4 · GaP 2.3 · GaAs 1.4 · GaSb 0.7 · AlN 6.3 · InSb 0.2 · ZnO 4.4 · ZnSe 2.7 · CdSe 1.7 · SiC 3.0; mixing tunes Eg.

Intermolecular forces

Strength: ion–ion > ion–dipole > H-bond > dipole–dipole > dispersion. Decision: ions? → H on N/O/F + LP? → polar shape? → else dispersion. Polar + nonpolar = dipole–induced dipole.
Dispersion ↑ with size/polarizability (CH₄ gas → C₁₇H₃₆ solid). BP rises with IMF: Ne < O₂ < N(CH₃)₃ < NaCl < CaO (2+/2− beats 1+/1−). HF high BP = H-bonds. IMF are between molecules, never covalent bonds.
V(r): ion–ion ∝ 1/r, ion–dipole 1/r², dipole–dipole 1/r³.

Structures

Diffraction

dhkl = a/√(h²+k²+l²) (cubic)   2d sinθ = nλ
θ = glancing angle; patterns plot 2θ → halve! Cu Kα 1.54 Å = 0.154 nm. Calc in degree mode. d220 = d110/2. r (atom), a (cell edge), d (plane spacing) are different.
Selection rules: SC all · BCC h+k+l even (110, 200, 211, 220…) · FCC all odd or all even (111, 200, 220, 311, 222…).
sin²θ ∝ h²+k²+l²: SC 1:2:3:4:5:6:8 · BCC 2:4:6:8:10:12 · FCC 3:4:8:11:12:16. Powder peaks give structure; peak angle gives a.
Example: λ=1.54 Å, Fe BCC a=2.866 → d₁₁₀=2.03, θ=22.3°, 2θ=44.7°.

Defects

Point (0D): vacancy · interstitial (small atom, C in Fe) · substitutional (similar size, Zn in Cu, dopants). Ionic: Schottky = cation + anion vacancy pair; Frenkel = ion moves to a gap (vacancy + interstitial, usually cation). Line (1D): edge dislocation ⊥ = extra half-plane; moves along slip plane → plastic deformation at 10³–10⁴× lower stress. Planar (2D): grain boundary; polycrystal vs single crystal.
Work hardening: more, tangled dislocations → harder, more brittle. Anything blocking dislocations hardens (alloy atoms, C in steel, hammering). Annealing: heat (no melting) → diffusion removes dislocations → softer, tougher.

Glass & polymers

Amorphous: no long-range order; forms if structure packs badly or cools fast. SiO₄ tetrahedron: Si–O 162 pm, 109.5°; quartz periodic, glass random. Network modifiers (Na, Ca) → non-bridging O → lower softening T (soda-lime < borosilicate < 96% silica < fused silica). No sharp melting: viscosity falls smoothly.
Faster cooling → higher molar volume, higher Tg; crystal drops suddenly at Tm. Polymers: monomers → chains; crosslink (S, vulcanization) → does not melt; HDPE semicrystalline.

Diffusion

J = −D dC/dx   D = D₀ exp(−Q/RT)   x ≈ √(Dt)
ln D vs 1/T: slope = −Q/R (steeper = larger Q; use J/mol). Q = R ln(D₂/D₁)/(1/T₁−1/T₂). D ↑ strongly with T. Interstitial (C, H in Fe): low Q, fast. Vacancy / self (Fe in Fe): high Q, Qv ↑ with Tm. Fick 2nd: ∂C/∂t = D ∂²C/∂x² (profile changes with time); surface at fixed C → erfc profile; depth ∝ √t.
Flux ×area = rate (doors: D·ΔC/Δx·A).
