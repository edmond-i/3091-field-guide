# 3.091 Field Guide

An unofficial, student-built study guide for **MIT 3.091: Introduction to Solid-State Chemistry (Fall 2026)**.

It is written for people who are starting from close to zero chemistry: every term is defined before it is used, every formula comes with the plain-language idea behind it, and the hard parts are drawn, not just described.

> Not affiliated with or endorsed by MIT or the 3.091 staff. The lectures, slides, and problem sets belong to their authors. This repo contains our own explanations, diagrams, and notes only. **Please never commit course PDFs, slides, exams, or graded work** (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## What is here

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`index.html`](index.html) | The Field Guide: one scrollable page with a table of contents, diagrams, worked examples, **"Check yourself" practice questions after every unit** (answers are checked instantly and progress is saved in your browser), practice archetypes, a formula sheet, and an exam-traps list. Light and dark mode, works on phones. |
| [`quiz-review.html`](quiz-review.html) | The quiz-prep page: every practice question grouped by what the quizzes ask, with recipes, typical slips, a retry list and timed 10-minute mock quizzes. Generated by `tools/build_practice.py`. |
| [`aid-sheet.html`](aid-sheet.html) / [`aid-sheet.pdf`](aid-sheet.pdf) | A dense one-page recall card sized for the course's allowed 8.5×11" exam aid sheet. |
| [`scripts/build-aid-sheet-pdf.sh`](scripts/build-aid-sheet-pdf.sh) | Rebuilds the PDF from the HTML (headless Chrome) and checks it is exactly one page. |
| [`tools/`](tools) | Python scripts that generate the diagrams as inline SVG (`gen_vsepr.py`, `gen_orbitals.py`, `gen_new_figures.py`, `gen_crystal_figures.py`) and the practice system: `practice_bank_*.py` (the questions), `practice_lib.py`, `practice.js`/`practice.css` (the checker) and `build_practice.py` (rebuilds the question blocks and `quiz-review.html`). Optional to run; the HTML files work on their own. |

## View it

No build step and no dependencies. Open `index.html` in a browser. (Web fonts load from Google Fonts; offline, the page falls back to system fonts.)

## Coverage

| Lectures | Topic | Guide section |
|---|---|---|
| L01-L03 | Atoms, history of atomic models, light, Bohr model | 01 |
| L04-L06 | Quantum numbers, orbitals, electron configurations, PES, periodic trends | 02 |
| L07-L08 | Octet rule, ionic bonding, Born-Haber, lattice energy, crystal structures | 03 |
| L09 | Covalent bonding, Lewis structures | 04 |
| L10-L11 | Covalent compound properties, VSEPR, polarity | 05 |
| L12 | Hybridization (built from scratch), molecular orbitals | 06 |
| L13 | Intermolecular forces: dipole-dipole, hydrogen bonds, dispersion | 07 |
| L14-L15 | Metals, energy bands, conductors/semiconductors/insulators, work function, electron-hole pairs, doping, compound semiconductors | 08 |
| L16-L17 | Early crystallography, lattices and motifs, 7 systems / 14 Bravais lattices, SC/BCC/FCC, close packing, holes and ionic structures, Miller indices, d-spacing, Bragg's law | 09 |
| L18-L19 | Point and line defects, alloys, dislocations, amorphous solids, silica glass, polymers | 10 |
| L20 | Diffusion: Fick's laws, activation energy, Exam 2 scope | 11 |
| all | Cheat codes, practice archetypes, formula sheet, exam traps | end of page |

Recitations 01-11 (including 9.5) and practice problem sets 01-03 are folded in as worked examples and "archetypes" (the question types that keep coming back).

**Not yet covered:** the Recitation 11 slide deck was unreadable (the Rec 11 solutions are covered), and material after Lecture 20 (kinetics, equilibrium, phase diagrams, organic) is not in the guide yet.

## Practice and quiz review

The practice questions are **our own** (modelled on the kinds of questions in past quizzes and recitations, with new numbers); they are not copies of any exam. Numeric answers in the question bank are computed in Python from the same numbers as the worked solution, so the checker and the text cannot disagree. To add a question, see "Adding practice questions" in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
